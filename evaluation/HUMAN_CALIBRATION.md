# Human Calibration

Before using a model judge on a holdout, have at least two reviewers grade an anonymous calibration subset against the same atomic criteria.

Record:

- reviewer instructions and source access;
- per-criterion labels and overall verdicts;
- disagreements and adjudication;
- judge-versus-human agreement;
- systematic verbosity, specificity, ordering, and abstention bias;
- rubric changes made before holdout use.

Do not expose model verdicts to human reviewers before they submit their labels.
