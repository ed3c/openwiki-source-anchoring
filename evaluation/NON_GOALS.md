# Non-goals

This framework does not claim that:

- the historical private target becomes publicly reproducible;
- one LLM judge establishes ground truth;
- lexical quote matching proves semantic entailment;
- a single repository or generation demonstrates generalization;
- permissively licensed evaluation software grants rights to every evaluated source, dataset, prompt, or output;
- a high aggregate score overrides a failed trust, licensing, security, or provenance gate.
