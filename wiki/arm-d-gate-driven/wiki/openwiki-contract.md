# The openwiki contract

`scripts/check_openwiki.py` validates "final repo openwiki usability and
lifecycle wiring" (src: scripts/check_openwiki.py `Validate final repo openwiki usability and lifecycle wiring.`).
It is a member of [`GATES`](../governance/git-gate.md), so a push that breaks the
documentation contract fails the same way a broken validator would.

Fourteen paths must exist (src: scripts/check_openwiki.py `REQUIRED_FILES = [`), and the
same three small-loop directories must not

## Two kinds of pinning

**Existence only.** `openwiki/quickstart.md` is the declared entry point and is
checked for existence alone, with the reason stated inline: it "is generated by
the wiki pipeline on every run, so pinning its content would freeze a generated
file" (src: scripts/check_openwiki.py `so pinning its content would freeze a generated`).

**Literal containment.** Every other page carries a list of strings that must
appear somewhere in it (src: scripts/check_openwiki.py `REQUIRED_LITERALS = {`), reported as
(src: scripts/check_openwiki.py `missing literal:`). The lists are specific: `README.md` must keep its
usage heading and gate command (src: scripts/check_openwiki.py `"python3 scripts/git_gate.py",`);
the lifecycle map must name its five phases including
(src: scripts/check_openwiki.py `"Weekly Ablation Audit & Retirement",`); the call-lifecycle page must
carry measured strings such as
(src: scripts/check_openwiki.py `"pr_golden_set.json: cases=4 passed=4",`); the workflow page must mention both
retrieval modes (src: scripts/check_openwiki.py `"GraphRAG",`).

(inferred) Containment, not equality, is the operative property, and it has a
practical consequence for anyone extending these pages: **adding** text is always
safe, **rewording** a pinned sentence is not. It also means a literal can survive
long after the fact it described has changed — the page can be simultaneously
gate-green and stale, which is why several of the hand-written pages carry
explicit corrections next to their pinned strings.

## The page that must never be hand-edited

`openwiki/nonofficial/structured-lifecycle-data.md` is pinned by literals here
(src: scripts/check_openwiki.py `"openwiki/nonofficial/structured-lifecycle-data.md": [`) *and* required to be
byte-identical to generated output by a different gate
(src: scripts/check_lifecycle_datasets.py `structured lifecycle openwiki must equal renderer output`). Any edit —

## How the generated wiki fits

`README.md` names `openwiki/quickstart.md` as the primary guide and describes
`openwiki/nonofficial/README.md` as "the index of the hand-written pages,
reachable from quickstart" (src: README.md `the index of the hand-written pages`).
The split is therefore explicit in the repository's own words: `nonofficial/`
holds human-authored, gate-pinned pages; everything else under `openwiki/` is
generated. Two other gates re-read the hand-written pages for their own literals
— (src: scripts/check_wiki_graph_sync.py `architecture page missing literal:`) and
(src: scripts/check_prompt_trace_assets.py `prompt trace openwiki missing literal:`) — and
`check_plan_package_compat.py` requires all of them to exist
(src: scripts/check_plan_package_compat.py `"openwiki/nonofficial/schema-standards.md",`).

(inferred) For a documentation pass this yields one rule worth stating loudly:
generated pages may be rewritten freely, `nonofficial/` may be extended but not
reworded, and the structured-lifecycle page may not be touched at all.
